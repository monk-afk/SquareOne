SERVER RULES

Staff may act on behavior that violates the spirit of these rules, even if not explicitly listed.

Use /rules in-game for a quick summary. This page provides additional clarification and examples.

Players are responsible for themselves, their items/pets, and their builds/areas.

  1. Conduct & Behavior
🚫 No real-life threats
  • Any threat involving real-world harm is taken seriously
🚫 No harassment or abuse
  • Targeted or repeated harassment is not allowed
🚫 No coordinated disruption
  • Includes group harassment or organized trolling
🚫 No manipulation of staff
  • Attempts to sabotage staff, or to undermine staff decisions are not allowed
  1. Chat & Communication
🚫 No sharing personal information
  • Includes your own or others identifying info
🚫 No filter evasion
  • Avoiding the curse filter intentionally is not allowed
🚫 No spam
  • Repeated or disruptive messages
🚫 No advertising
  • Do not promote other servers
🚫 No adult topics or explicit content
  • Keep chat appropriate for general audiences (E10+)
  1. Gameplay
🚫 No exploiting
  • Bugs, glitches, or modified clients
🚫 No abusive PvP behavior
  • Includes spawn killing, camping, or serial targeting
🚫 No begging
  • Repeated requests for items or privileges
🚫 No misuse of protection blocks
  • Protections should not be used to trap, block unfairly, or grief

Have Fun, Stay Safe!
