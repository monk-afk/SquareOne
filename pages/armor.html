Per-piece durability

Select armor to calculate durability.

    Browse all armor pieces

    The reference durability column assumes five equipped armor elements. Physics columns are the modifiers added by each piece.

    Defense Attributes

    • durability Total survivable hits until item breaks.
      • Received damage to armor is divided by total number of equipped armor pieces
    • defense Percentage reduction to incoming damage.
    • block Percentage chance of receiving zero damage from a hit.
    • reflect Causes counter damage to the attacker's weapon.
    • fire resist Protection against fire and lava.

    Physics Modifiers

    Base values are 1.0. Each selected piece adds its positive or negative modifier.

    • jump Your jump-strength multiplier.
    • speed Your walking and sprinting-speed multiplier.
    • gravity Your downward-gravity multiplier.
